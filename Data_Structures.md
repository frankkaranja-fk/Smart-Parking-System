

|Data structure|Where Used|Reason for use|
|-|-|-|
|Array|Storing all parking list|Fast access by index and easy to display availability|
|Queue|Vehicles waiting at entry|First-Come-First-Served order is natural for entry|
|Dictionary|Looking up vehicle by number plate|Very fast search when a car wants to exit|
|Struct|Vehicle record and slot record|Groups related data(plate, entry time etc)|
|Linked List|History of all transactions|Easy to add new records without fixed size|
